About Fairfield Education Center

Committed to providing the highest quality childcare and education

Our Story

Established in 2010, Fairfield Education Center was founded on the belief that every child deserves access to exceptional, nurturing childcare and education. What started as a small center with one location has grown into a trusted network of four learning communities serving families across our region.

Our founders envisioned a place where children wouldn't just be cared for, but truly nurtured and celebrated. Today, we remain committed to that vision—creating warm, welcoming environments where each child feels valued and supported in their unique journey of growth and learning.

Our Mission

Our mission at Fairfield Education Center is to provide the highest level of care to every child who comes through our doors. We believe that every child deserves the very best care, and we are dedicated to serving the needs of our children, families, employees, and communities. Our state-of-the-art facilities and highly qualified and caring team create a warm and welcoming atmosphere where children can learn, grow, and thrive.

Our Vision

We envision a world where every child has access to exceptional early education that supports their full potential. We are committed to being a trusted and reliable resource for families in our community, fostering not just learning but genuine love for discovery, curiosity, and growth in every child we serve.
