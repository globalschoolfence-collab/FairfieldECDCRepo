Frequently Asked Questions

What are your center hours?

We're open Monday through Friday from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Hours may vary by location.

How do I schedule a tour?

You can schedule a tour by emailing info@fairfieldec.org or using our contact form. Tours are typically available during business hours.

What is your enrollment process?

Our enrollment process involves submitting an application, scheduling a tour, completing required documents, and paying the registration fee. Most families complete the process within 1-2 weeks.

What does enrollment include?

Enrollment includes daily care, meals and snacks, educational programs, activities, outdoor play, and regular communication with parents about your child's progress.

Do you offer flexible scheduling options?

Yes! We offer Full-Time, Part-Time, and Drop-In options to fit your family's needs. Pricing varies by schedule type.

What if my child has food allergies?

We take allergies very seriously. We'll work with you to create safe meal plans and train staff on your child's specific allergies and emergency protocols.

Do you offer financial assistance?

Yes! We accept state subsidies and voucher programs. Contact us to discuss financial assistance options available in your area.

Can parents visit during the day?

Absolutely! We encourage parent involvement. We ask that you schedule visits in advance to minimize disruption to classroom activities.
