Our Programs

Programs Designed for Growth

Each program is carefully designed to support your child's development at every stage

Infant Care

Ages 6 weeks - 12 months

Program Focus

  • Secure attachments and bonding
  • Sensory development and exploration
  • Language and communication skills
  • Motor skills development
  • Individual care routines and schedules

Daily Activities

  • Responsive caregiving and feeding
  • Tummy time and movement activities
  • Sensory play with safe materials
  • Singing and music exploration
  • Outdoor time in strollers
  • Diaper changes and hygiene routines

Program Highlights

Our infant program prioritizes responsive care, where caregivers are attuned to each baby's individual needs and cues. We maintain small group sizes to ensure personalized attention and secure attachments. Parents receive daily updates on eating, sleeping, and developmental milestones.

Toddler Program

Ages 12 months - 3 years

Program Focus

  • Language development and vocabulary building
  • Gross and fine motor skill development
  • Social skills and peer interaction
  • Independence and self-help skills
  • Creative exploration and play

Daily Activities

  • Language-rich interactive play
  • Climbing and gross motor activities
  • Art and sensory exploration
  • Story time and music
  • Outdoor play and gross motor games
  • Simple routines and transitions

Program Highlights

Our toddler program emphasizes active exploration, language development, and social learning. We provide safe spaces for children to practice independence while maintaining consistent routines. Teachers model language and encourage peer interaction while supervising play to ensure safety.

Preschool Program

Ages 3 - 5 years

Program Focus

  • Pre-literacy and early math skills
  • Science and nature exploration
  • Creative arts and self-expression
  • Social-emotional development
  • Critical thinking and problem-solving

Daily Activities

  • Circle time and group discussions
  • Literacy and numeracy activities
  • Science experiments and exploration
  • Arts and crafts projects
  • Outdoor play and sports
  • Cooperative games and team activities

Program Highlights

Our preschool program uses play-based learning to build foundational academic skills. We incorporate thematic units, hands-on activities, and meaningful group experiences. Teachers facilitate discovery and encourage creativity while gently introducing pre-academic concepts.

Pre-Kindergarten Program

Ages 4 - 5 years

Program Focus

  • Academic readiness and skills
  • Reading and writing foundations
  • Mathematical reasoning
  • Independence and self-regulation
  • Responsibility and leadership skills

Daily Activities

  • Structured academic lessons
  • Reading and writing practice
  • Math and problem-solving activities
  • Library and research skills
  • Field trips and real-world learning
  • Leadership and buddy roles

Program Highlights

Our pre-K program prepares children for kindergarten success. We focus on academic skills, independence, and self-regulation. Our curriculum aligns with kindergarten standards while maintaining a playful, engaging approach to learning and exploration.

Before/After School Program

Ages 5 - 12 years

Program Focus

  • Academic support and homework help
  • Social skills and friendship building
  • Recreation and physical activity
  • Enrichment programs and clubs
  • Safe, supervised environment

Daily Activities

  • Homework support and tutoring
  • Sports and recreational activities
  • Arts, crafts, and STEM projects
  • Snack time and social time
  • Outdoor play and games
  • Community service and leadership

Program Highlights

Our before and after school program provides safe, nurturing care for school-age children. We offer homework support, enriching activities, and plenty of time for social interaction. Children develop new skills and friendships while parents have peace of mind.

Summer Camp Program

Ages 3 - 12 years | June - August

Program Focus

  • Themed weekly activities
  • Field trips and adventures
  • Arts and creative projects
  • Sports and outdoor fun
  • Social skills and friendships

Daily Activities

  • Weekly themed activities
  • Field trips to local attractions
  • Water play and outdoor activities
  • Arts and craft projects
  • Sports clinics and games
  • Special summer events

Program Highlights

Our summer camp offers exciting, themed experiences designed to keep children engaged and active. With weekly themes, field trips, and a variety of activities, summer becomes an adventure. Children develop new skills, make new friends, and create lasting summer memories.
