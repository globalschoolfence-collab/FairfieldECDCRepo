Our Mission

Our mission at Fairfield Education Center is to provide the highest level of care to every child who comes through our doors. We believe that every child deserves the very best care, and we are dedicated to serving the needs of our children, families, employees, and communities. Our state-of-the-art facilities and highly qualified and caring team create a warm and welcoming atmosphere where children can learn, grow, and thrive. We are committed to being a trusted and reliable resource for families and are always working to improve and expand our services. We look forward to serving you and your child as they embark on their journey of growth and learning.

More Than Just a Daycare

⭐

5-Star Programs

Our centers are not just ordinary childcare facilities, but places where your child's holistic development is nurtured and celebrated. We're proud to have received 5-star ratings, a testament to our unwavering commitment to offering the highest quality programs.

🍎

Wholesome Nutritional Offerings

Beyond just care and education, we understand the importance of proper nutrition in your child's development. Prepared in our own kitchens, we serve meals that are fresh and hot, designed with a healthy balance of nutrients adhering to USDA standards.

🏫

State-of-the-Art Facilities

Our facilities are vibrant, welcoming spaces designed with an abundance of large windows letting in natural sunlight. We feature both indoor and outdoor play areas, computer labs, and a host of other amenities for optimal learning experiences.

👨‍🏫

In-House Educator Training

Our commitment to providing the best education includes continual professional development for our educators. We provide training and credentialing support so your child will always be learning from the best, most up-to-date, and passionate educators.
